# Privacy Policy for 636mecheri (100% Offline Utility)

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026  
**Package ID:** `com.mecheri636.offline`

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Network Access
`636mecheri` is a 100% offline Android application. It does **not** declare or use `INTERNET`, Wi-Fi, Mobile Data, Bluetooth, Location, or USB networking permissions. The app sends **0 bytes** to any server, developer, or third party.

## 2. Camera Permission Usage
The app requests only the Android `CAMERA` permission exclusively for **local, on-device QR code scanning** (reading shop price lists, buyer carts, and offline receipts). No camera frames, photos, or videos are ever recorded, stored, or transmitted.

## 3. Local Storage Only
All shop catalogs, prices, carts, expenses, and receipts are stored strictly inside your device's private local SQLite database. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local app data.
